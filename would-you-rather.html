A little game for your next hangout.

  • Grab your friends.
  • Choose a category or go random.
  • Take turns answering.
  • There are no right answers.

How to play

  1. 01Choose a category— or let us surprise you.
  2. 02Read the question out loud.
  3. 03Everyone chooses an answerand explains why.
  4. 04Press next and keep going.

No right answers. No wrong answers. Just good conversations.
